# Resolve Complex Merge Conflicts and Document Strategies

## Task Brief
Practice resolving multi-file conflicts across branches; establish and document rebase vs merge decision framework in repository wiki.

## Scenario
Your team needs conflict resolution guidelines and real-world practice resolving complex multi-branch conflicts safely.

## Deliverables
- Resolved conflicts across 3+ branches
- Wiki page documenting merge strategy

## Success Criteria
- All conflicts resolved without data loss
- Clear rebase vs merge decision criteria documented
